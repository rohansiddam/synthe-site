Self-improving AI agents

Your AI agent gets better while you sleep.

You say what good looks like. Synthe tests changes overnight and keeps only what wins. It works on your agent's prompt, in your own GitHub repo, while you're away. In the morning, a report says what changed and why.

$npx synthe init
