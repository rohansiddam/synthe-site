Your agents, improving in your own repo

Synthe is a self-improvement engine for AI agents. As a design partner, you run the loop on your own agents, in your own repo, against your own goal, and your team reviews every change until the loop has earned more room.

Talk to us Get started

What a pilot looks like

One agent, your repo, your bar. A pilot runs the full loop on a production agent while your team stays in charge of what good means.

What you provide

A repo with an agent you already run in production. The checks that say what good looks like. And the goal you want the agent to reach: a number you can measure, or a quality your team can judge.

What Synthe does

Synthe sets up in the repo with one command. Each round, it finds where the agent falls short, proposes a fix, tests it on simulated messages against your checks, and merges only what clearly does better. A change that stops winning can be reverted.

What you see

Every change as a pull request with its evidence attached. Every morning, a report on every round: what was tried, what it scored, why it was kept or dropped, and what the round cost alongside its result.

Review first. Autonomy is earned.

Nothing merges on its own at the start. The dial is yours, and it moves only as far as the evidence earns.

At the start

Your team approves every merge

Synthe proposes a change and tests it against your checks and a held-out set. The pull request carries the evidence. A person on your team reads it and decides whether it merges.

As evidence builds

Clear winners can merge on their own

When the record shows the loop finding real improvements, you can let Synthe merge clear winners by itself and keep reviewing the rest. Every merge still lands as a pull request, still reversible, and still in the next morning’s report.

Teams already running agents

Synthe is goal-agnostic and system-agnostic. It runs on any agentic system your team already operates, against any goal you pick, quantitative or qualitative: a support agent resolving more of its tickets, an outreach agent reaching more of the right people, a returns agent that follows the policy every time.

Set it up yourself npx synthe@latest init

The tests improve too

It improves the agent itself, and it improves the testing that judges the agent. The checks get sharper as the agent gets better, so the bar means more with every round.
