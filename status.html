Status & trust

Plain facts about what Synthe is, what it isn't, what we haven't proven yet, and what this site does with your information.

Project status

  • StageEarly. Synthe is the commit barrier for AI agents. Its checker, integrations and commit broker are published at github.com/rohansiddam/Synthe. The spec, checker, clients and integrations are open source under Apache-2.0. The commit broker is source-available under FSL-1.1 and in beta, with design partners helping shape it (how it's licensed).
  • Public todayThe checker, v0.5: signed handoffs, approvals that pin their target, pinned evidence, wait-for dependencies, exclusive paths, and an exactly-once claim ledger. 753 public tests pass.
  • Public betaThe commit broker, public since October 7, 2026. It holds the credentials, re-checks each action at the moment of commit, pushes with compare-and-swap, and signs a receipt for every allow and every deny. git_push ships first.
  • Proven in betaOn October 7, 2026, on a real Mac with real GitHub: the broker ran as its own hidden user and the agent in its own account with no GitHub access, and the setup check passed all 8 of its checks. A live red team of 33 attacks from the agent's account was refused or contained (33 of 33), each refusal with a signed receipt.
  • ProvenExactly-once claims on real GitHub runners (receipts); all 8 shipped attacks rejected with named reason codes.
  • Not yetNo hosted service and no SLA. You run Synthe where your agents run.

What we haven't proven yet

  • Model behaviorIn our first run with a real model (an OpenClaw agent), it followed the rules, proposed its push and stopped for approval. One run is not a measurement. A refusal stops the action either way.
  • Real-world speedWe only quote speed numbers we measured ourselves, and we haven't measured the overhead against real GitHub pushes yet.
  • macOS isolationThe broker's isolation is verified on Linux, with two real users, in Docker, and, since October 7, 2026, on macOS with the setup's own installer. Setup on a fresh Mac by someone new hasn't been timed yet.

What Synthe can't stop

  • Approved actionsAn action a human approved will do exactly what was approved. Synthe narrows a compromised sender to that one action; it can't remove it.
  • Which model wrote itReceipts prove which key and which connector acted, not which model produced the work.
  • Agents that go around itIf an agent still holds its own push rights, the barrier is optional. Take those credentials away.
  • Root on the same machineOn a shared laptop in same-user dev mode, Synthe is a seatbelt, not a wall, and every receipt says so. Root, passwordless sudo and the docker group defeat any isolation.

What it is not

  • Not a certificateSynthe produces the evidence auditors and underwriters ask for. It doesn't make anyone certified, compliant or insured.
  • Not an orchestratorSynthe doesn't run or schedule your agents. It's the commit step for whatever does.
  • Not a blockchainNo chain, no tokens, no consensus. Signed packets, a registry, a ledger and hash-chained receipts.
  • Not tamper-proofTamper-evident. Synthe detects alteration and names the broken link; it can't stop someone from altering their own copy.
  • Not a truth machineIt checks that evidence matches its pinned hash, not that the evidence itself is true.
  • Not an endorsementCompany names on the homepage show compatibility only: anything that emits JSON works. No endorsement or partnership is implied.

Your data

  • This site collectsNothing on its own. No accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels. Fonts and scripts are served from synthe.live itself, so loading a page sends no request anywhere else.
  • The demosThe race, forge, playground and tamper demos run entirely in your browser. Demo keys never leave the tab.
  • The partner formRequesting access opens your email app with your request ready to send. Nothing is stored or sent on its own.
  • Light or darkIf you pick a theme, the choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

Security contact

Found a flaw in the checker, the broker or the threat model? Email rohansiddam@synthe.live first, and give us a chance to fix it before you publish. For anything that isn't a security issue, GitHub issues are fine. Our security.txt says the same.
