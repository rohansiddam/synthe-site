Limits and your data

Limits, and where your data goes

Plain facts about what Synthe doesn't do, and what happens to your prompt and anything you send this site.

Status

  • StageEarly. Synthe is open source under Apache-2.0 at github.com/synthelabs/synthe.
  • Not yetNo hosted service, no SLA, no price list.

Limits

  • Simulated testsChanges are tested on simulated messages, not real users. Check the results yourself.
  • Prompt onlyOnly your agent's prompt changes. Your code and tools are never run.
  • Your branchWins can merge straight into your default branch, or wait in a pull request for your review. Every change can be reverted.

Your data

  • Where it runsIn your repo. Setup runs on your computer; rounds run in your repo's GitHub Actions. With an event code, model calls go through Synthe's capped proxy.
  • Your codes or keySaved as repo secrets: SYNTHE_EVENT_CODE and SYNTHE_TEAM_CODE, or SYNTHE_ANTHROPIC_KEY. Your own key always wins over event credit.
  • Sharing at the eventDuring the event, your prompt, test cases and how the run goes are shared with the Synthe team, never your code. Set "share": "none" in synthe.json to turn it off.
  • What is sentYour prompt, the code around it and the test messages go to Anthropic's API to be tested. Synthe never runs your agent's code.

This site

  • This site collectsNothing on its own. No accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels. Fonts and scripts are served from synthe.live itself.
  • The contact formIt opens your email app with a note ready to send. Nothing is stored or sent on its own.
  • Light or darkIf you pick a theme, the choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

Security contact

Found a security problem in Synthe? Email rohansiddam@synthe.live first, and give us a chance to fix it before you publish. For anything else, GitHub issues are fine. Our security.txt says the same.
