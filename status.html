Status & trust

Plain facts about what Synthe is, what it is not, and what this site does with your information.

Project status

  • StageEarly, open, and evaluable. The checker, spec, threat model, and tests are public at github.com/rohansiddam/Synthe, shared under an evaluation license.
  • ProvenExactly-once semantics on real GitHub runners (receipts); eight shipped attacks all rejected with named reason codes.
  • Not yetNo hosted service, no managed keys, no SLA. You run the checker where your agents run.

What it is not

  • Not a blockchainNo chain, no tokens, no consensus. A signed packet checked against a registry and a local ledger.
  • Not tamper-proofTamper-evident. Synthe detects alteration and names the broken link; it cannot prevent someone from altering their own copy.
  • Not a truth machineIt checks that evidence matches its pinned hash, not that the evidence itself is true.
  • Not an endorsementFramework and model logos on the homepage indicate compatibility only. No endorsement is implied.

Your data

  • This site collectsNothing. No accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels.
  • The demosThe forge, playground, and tamper demo run entirely in your browser. Demo keys never leave the tab.
  • The contact formRequesting access opens your email app with a draft. Nothing is stored or sent automatically.

Security contact

Found a flaw in the checker or the threat model? Open an issue at github.com/rohansiddam/Synthe/issues or write to info@synthe.live.

Synthe is an early project, shared under an evaluation license.
